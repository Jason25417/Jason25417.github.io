I'm Jason Tu, an undergraduate at The Ohio State University in Columbus, studying mathematics (honors, theory track) and data analytics.

I split my time between proofs and software. On the mathematics side I co-authored a paper in dynamical systems. On the software side I contribute to a Rust web framework and ship apps for Apple platforms. The [work page](/work/) has all of it.

## How I work

- **I build things I will use every day.** A project has succeeded when it earns a place in my own routine.
- **Failures should be visible.** A system that quietly degrades is worse than one that stops and says why.
- **Prior art first.** Before building, I look for what already exists and for the proper name of the problem. When I fix something in someone else's project, the fix goes back upstream.
- **One model, few special cases.** When a design grows a separate branch for every scenario, I rebuild it around a single idea.
- **Quality before speed.** I would rather get one thing right and then make it fast.
- **AI does much of the typing; the judgment stays with me.** I hand a lot of execution to AI agents and keep decisions and acceptance for myself. How much I hand over depends on [whether the action can be undone](/notes/delegating-by-reversibility/). Whatever I ship, I should be able to explain line by line.
